        Theme: Artificial Intelligence (AI) learning portal.

        Challenge: design and develop an interactive AI learning portal for high school students (grades 9–12) that demystifies AI with a foundational understanding of the technology, showcases practical tools, and teaches techniques to use AI effectively and ethically in an academic setting.
